These terms apply when you request a quote, sign up for a plan, or book a cleanup with Scoop 757, operated by Entemps LLC ("we", "us"). By signing up or booking, you agree to them.

The service

We remove dog waste from your yard on a regular schedule, bag it, and take it with us. Weekly plans include one visit per week, on the same weekday when possible.

Plans and billing

  • Weekly plans are $79/mo for 1 dog, $95/mo for 2 dogs, and $110/mo for 3 dogs. Each dog beyond 3 is +$15/mo. Twice-weekly service, one-time cleanups, deodorizer add-ons, and HOA work are quoted separately.
  • Plans are billed monthly in advance through Stripe, starting after a 7-day free trial that covers your first weekly visit.
  • If a payment fails, we may pause service until it is resolved.
  • We will give you at least 30 days' notice by text or email before changing your price.

Free first cleanup

New weekly customers get their first cleanup free, however long it has been since the yard was cleaned. One per household. It doesn't apply to one-time cleanups or HOA work.

Cancelling

There is no contract. You can cancel anytime by text, call, or email. Cancel during the 7-day trial and you won't be charged. Cancel after that and service continues through the end of the month you've paid for. We don't give partial-month refunds.

Your yard and your dogs

  • Please give us access on service day: an unlocked gate or a gate code.
  • Keep dogs inside during our visit if they are not friendly with visitors. If a dog appears unsafe, we'll skip the visit, text you, and come back once it's safe.
  • We latch your gate when we leave and text you a photo. Please tell us about any gate that doesn't close properly.
  • Let us know about hazards in the yard, such as holes, sprinkler heads, or anything we shouldn't step on.

Weather and missed visits

If storms or unsafe conditions push a visit back, we come the next clear day. If we can't get into the yard because of a locked gate, an unsafe dog, or a blocked yard, the visit counts as completed.

Problems

If something was missed, text us within 24 hours and we'll come back and fix it at no charge. We carry liability insurance for work on your property. Please tell us about any damage within 48 hours of a visit.

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. Changes that affect your price follow the notice rule above.
