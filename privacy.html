Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Scoop 757 is a dog waste removal service in Virginia Beach and Chesapeake, Virginia, operated by Entemps LLC ("we", "us"). This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Quote requests: your name, phone number, email (optional), street address, ZIP code, number of dogs, service type, and any notes you add, such as a gate code.
  • Payments: when you sign up for a plan, Stripe collects and processes your card details. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Service records: visit dates and the gate photos we text you after each visit.
  • Calls and texts: messages you send to (757) 453-5527.

How we use it

  • To give you a price, schedule visits, and reach your yard.
  • To text you visit updates and gate photos.
  • To bill your plan and send receipts.
  • To answer your questions.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not send marketing texts unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the business:

  • Stripe, for payments and receipts.
  • Web3Forms, which delivers quote requests from this website to our email.
  • Our email and phone providers, to receive your messages.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Gate codes and access details

We use gate codes and access notes only to service your yard. We keep them private and delete them when you cancel.

How long we keep it

We keep customer records while you use our service and for as long as tax and accounting rules require afterward. Quote requests that don't become customers are deleted within 12 months.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete your information at any time. Text or call (757) 453-5527 or email entempsllc@gmail.com. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe's checkout pages may use cookies for security and fraud prevention.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
