Questions

Do I need to be home?

No. Just leave the gate unlocked or share the code. Please keep your dogs inside during our visit if they aren't friendly with visitors.

What do you do with the waste?

We bag it and haul it away. Nothing is left in your trash can.

Is there a contract?

No. Service is month to month and you can cancel anytime.

How do I pay?

Sign up with a card through our secure Stripe checkout and you're billed monthly in advance. You'll get a receipt by email each month.

What if it's been a while since the yard was cleaned?

No judgment. Your first cleanup is free for new weekly customers, however long it's been.

Do you service HOAs and apartment communities?

Yes. We clean common areas and empty pet waste stations. Request a quote and we'll visit the property.
